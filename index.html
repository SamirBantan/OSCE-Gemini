Learning Objectives

  • Master structured clinical reasoning under authentic 8-minute time constraints.
  • Prioritise high-yield diagnostic investigations and safe initial management plans.
  • Formulate key differential diagnoses for red-flag medical emergencies.
  • Practice verbal fluency using Speech-to-Text keyword analysis.

Station Protocol

  1. Read Examiner Brief: Review the candidate instruction outside the room.
  2. Review Scenario & Vitals: Analyse baseline parameters and physical findings.
  3. Step-by-Step Decisions: Work through History, Exam, Tests, Diagnosis, & Rx.
  4. Verbal Practice Mode: Use the mic to state your findings out loud for Speech Recognition evaluation.
  5. Review Pearls: Read targeted teaching feedback on completed options.

5-Step Station Workflow

1. History Taking High-Yield Questions
2. Physical Examination Targeted Signs
3. Investigations Bedside & Bloods
4. Diagnosis & Differential Working Dx
5. Management Plan Immediate Actions

WIPE Opening Routine

Essential first 30 seconds inside any OSCE station.

  • W: Wash hands thoroughly
  • I: Introduce yourself and role
  • P: Patient identification (Name + DOB)
  • E: Explain procedure & gain consent
2

SOCRATES Pain History

Systematic analysis for pain presentation.

• Site
• Onset
• Character
• Radiation
• Associations
• Time course
• Exacerbating
• Severity (1-10)

8-Minute Time Pacing

Structuring your station duration effectively.

  • Minute 0-1: Brief & WIPE
  • Minute 1-5: Targeted Exam / History
  • Minute 5-6 text: Synthesis & Tests
  • Minute 7-8: Structured Presentation
4

Sepsis Six Emergency Rule

Deliver within 1 hour of identifying severe sepsis.

3 TAKE: Blood cultures, Lactate, Urine Output
3 GIVE: High-flow O₂, IV Antibiotics, IV Fluids
5

Fluids & Handover Format

Examiner synthesis statement model.

"In summary, this is a [Age]-year-old presenting with [Chief Complaint]. Examination reveals [Key Signs]. My leading diagnostic hypothesis is [Primary Dx], with [Differential] as key alternatives. I would order [Key Tests] and initiate [Immediate Rx]."

  • • Specify timeframes for routine follow-up.
